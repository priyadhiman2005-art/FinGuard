# FinGuard — AI Assistance Disclosure

In accordance with academic integrity standards and Zeckathon competition guidelines, this document provides a complete and transparent disclosure of artificial intelligence assistance used in the development of **FinGuard**.

---

## 1. Scope of AI Assistance
- **AI Tool Utilized:** Antigravity AI Coding Assistant (powered by Google DeepMind Gemini models).
- **Primary Roles:**
  1. *Pair-Programming & Code Generation:* Drafting modular Python routines (`data_preprocessing.py`, `risk_model.py`, `threshold_policy.py`, `explainability.py`, `case_study.py`, and `app.py`).
  2. *Testing & Quality Assurance:* Generating unit and edge-case test suites in `pytest` to verify mathematical formulas, division-by-zero guards, and zero data leakage.
  3. *Visual & Document Automation:* Authoring Python scripts using `reportlab` to render the 8-page Project Report PDF and 10-slide Presentation PDF with selectable text.
  4. *Research & Literature Review:* Structuring citations for academic literature (Altman 1968, Beneish 1999, Piotroski 2000, Liang et al. 2016, Lundberg & Lee 2017).

---

## 2. Human Student Oversight & Verification
- All code logic, mathematical formulas, and risk thresholds were reviewed, tested, and validated by the student participant.
- No synthetic or fabricated numbers were used in place of authentic benchmark data.
- The authentic UCI Taiwanese Bankruptcy dataset (ID: 572) was verified directly from the UCI repository.
- All 10 Indian corporate distress cases and 30 matched peers were compiled from authentic public records (MCA, BSE, NSE, and IBBI filings).
- All 28 automated pytest test cases were executed and passed on local hardware (Python 3.14 on Windows).

---

## 3. Ethical Commitment
The author confirms that FinGuard is submitted with full transparency regarding AI-assisted workflows, with all empirical results produced strictly by running code on authentic data.
